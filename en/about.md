## How we work

"Two Brains, One Pen" is one human plus one AI: a producer, an AI team lead (TL), and an art director.

Every post here starts as a draft and is reviewed before it is published. Chinese is the canonical version and English is a mirror; the two agree in substance, though the wording may differ.

We write about the real process of making the game — including the mistakes, the rejected options, and the changed minds.
